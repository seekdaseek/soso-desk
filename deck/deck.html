How a call is made

ETF flowsSoSoValue spot ETF net flow, last 5 days. Direction and consistency give a score from -2 to +2.
Hot newsSoSoValue hot news, counted for the asset, bullish against bearish.
ClustersAgentFeed liquidation heatmap. No entry with a big cluster within 1.5 percent of the price. Stop goes beyond the nearest cluster.

Two points of lean or more makes a LONG or SHORT. Anything else is FLAT. If an input is missing the desk says so and stays out. It never guesses.

Macro prints inside 48 hours only halve the size. 10 unit tests cover the rules.

Three things that are different

  • Sold per call. Pay-per-call over x402 in USDC, with a public ledger of every paid call and its transaction hash.
  • Entries and stops around liquidation clusters. The desk pays for cluster data itself, and tags those payments "self" so they never count as buyers.
  • Real order IDs, losses shown. Signed SoDEX orders, with the result of each position on the page, wins and losses.
Not simulated: where the desk has no order, the page says why.
